A VARIED LANDSCAPE

Three sides of island life

Sandy and rocky shorelines, lush tropical forest, and a mountainous interior all fit within Taniti’s compact landscape.

THE COAST

Along the bay

White sandy beaches curve around Yellow Leaf Bay near Taniti City. The island also has rocky beaches, snorkeling, and chartered fishing tours.

THE RAINFOREST

A greener interior

Taniti’s lush rainforest draws visitors for hikes and zip-lining, with boat and bus tours offering other ways to see the island.

THE HIGHLANDS

A small active volcano

The mountainous interior includes Taniti’s active volcano, one of the island’s popular sightseeing destinations.

PLACES TO FIND YOUR PACE

Two places to start exploring

THE ISLAND’S LARGEST CITY

Taniti City

Native architecture, shopping, restaurants, and local culture sit close to the white-sand beaches around Yellow Leaf Bay. The city is fairly flat and easy to explore on foot.

NORTH OF YELLOW LEAF BAY

Merriton Landing

A rapidly developing area with many visitor activities. Its walkable setting brings dining, lodging, and entertainment close together.

ROOTED IN THE ISLAND

A community shaped by land and sea

Taniti is home to an indigenous population of about 20,000. Fishing and agriculture have long supported the local economy; tourism has become a growing part of island life.

Local flavors

Ten restaurants serve a mix of local fish and rice, American-style meals, and Pan-Asian cuisine. Local history, art, and island traditions offer another way to connect with Taniti.

BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

Good to know

A few practical details can make getting around and settling in easier.

Getting around

Public buses serve Taniti City from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. daily; private buses serve the rest of the island. Taxis operate in the city, and rental cars are available near the airport.

Money

Taniti uses U.S. dollars. Many businesses also accept euros, yen, and major credit cards; banks offer currency exchange.

Language

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. English is less common in rural areas, especially among older residents.

Health & safety

One hospital and several clinics serve the island, and the hospital has multilingual employees. Violent crime is very rare, though visitors should be aware of pickpocketing and other petty theft.

Planning around holidays

Taniti observes many national holidays. Tourist attractions and restaurants may close, so check schedules when planning your visit.
