COAST & WATER

Beaches of Yellow Leaf Bay

Taniti’s coastline includes both sandy and rocky beaches. Near Taniti City, white sandy beaches encircle Yellow Leaf Bay, a popular area for visitors.

Make a day of it

  • Choose between sandy shoreline and more rugged coastal stretches.
  • Snorkeling and chartered fishing tours are among the island’s water activities.
  • Taniti City is close to the bay, dining, and local architecture.

GREEN ISLAND INTERIOR

Into the rainforest

Lush tropical rainforest covers part of Taniti’s varied terrain. Exploring the rainforest is one of the main reasons visitors come to the island.

Ways to explore

  • Take a hike through the rainforest.
  • Try zip-lining for a different view of the forest.
  • Island boat and bus tours offer other ways to see Taniti.

MOUNTAINOUS INTERIOR

Visit Taniti’s volcano

Taniti’s mountainous interior is home to a small active volcano. Visiting the volcano is one of the island’s popular sightseeing activities.

Build it into your trip

  • Boat and bus tours travel around the island.
  • Rainforest hikes and helicopter rides are also available activities.
  • Check local attraction schedules before you go; many close on national holidays.
